1. Bottom line

No implementation defect that changes any reported number was established. Every numerical claim I could regenerate from source reproduced exactly, and the raw-to-summary chain, the original-summary comparisons and the generated tables all check out. The two confirmed defects are a missing record of solver status in four runners (no fallback actually occurred) and a latent default in an unused code path.

The real problems are in interpretation and disclosure:


The report's main text says stacking reaches 85.0% at bandwidth multiplier 0.25 and "approaches LGCP". That figure comes from a probe that rescaled only the final fit while keeping stacking weights learned at the default bandwidth. With every nested prefix rescaled (the C4 rule, same 0.25 scale at every origin), stacking gets 82.5%, and at origin 7 it gets 77.2% instead of 92.1%.
C1 reports only stacked versus pooled. In 3 of 5 regimes, a fixed single component (trend or recent) beats stacking with a paired interval that excludes zero, by up to 6.8 pp.
In C5, the transport and TV radii are set in 20/20 seeds (4 of 6 regimes) by the first backtest. At that prefix, stacking has no validation data and silently becomes pooled history. W1 radii run 3 to 13 times the realized forecast-to-truth distance, so the negative W1 result depends on this calibration rule.
A stale validation record (118 tests, 9 result sets) is still what README and START_HERE point readers to.


Merge blockers: none in the code. Two documentation items (D1, D6) are factual inaccuracies in shipped text. I would fix them before merge. That decision is yours.

Severity scale: High (a wrong headline or result), Medium (a misleading claim or a material interpretive gap), Low (housekeeping or hardening).

Confirmed defects

D3 (Low). Solver fallback status is not recorded in four runners.


Where: src/stochplace/robustness.py:92-108, scripts/transport_study.py:79-98, scripts/sampler_study.py:51-57 (and the prefix fits at research_samplers.py:43), and scripts/ais_research.py:207 (records weight_fit but not weight_fit_message).

Fix: add weight_fit and weight_fit_message columns to these rows (new columns only), and have the counter print "not recorded" for studies that lack them.


D4 (Low, latent). _epochs defaults to the wrong origin.


Where: src/stochplace/ais.py:278.
Evidence: when origin is None, it falls back to the midnight of the first line's start time, not the first raw report's midnight. With 5-hour epochs and reports that begin before midnight, that shifts the lattice relative to extraction; I demonstrated the index shift. Every caller in the repository passes origin, so no result is affected.
Fix: make origin required, or raise when it is None.

Proposed method changes (new preregistered work, not replacements)

P1 (Low). Stacking fallback.


Where: forecast.py:508-517.
Proposal: when SLSQP fails, compare the held-out objective at the projected SLSQP iterate and at the pseudo-BMA weights, or retry with a second simplex solver, and record both. Exit mode 8 often stops near the optimum.


P2 (Medium). C5 radius calibration confound.


Where: scripts/transport_study.py:45-54.
Evidence: in drift, lane_shift, reversal and seam_drift, the target 5 error sets the radius in 20/20 seeds. Its mean W1 error is 1.64, 4.98, 2.01 and 0.75, against roughly 0.15 to 0.79 at targets 6 and 7; TV shows the same pattern. Target 5 uses a prefix of 4 epochs, where stacking has no backtest and returns pooled (insufficient_history, 120/120). So the radius measures the error of pooled history, not of the 8-epoch stacked plan it protects. Median ratios of W1 radius to the realized truth distance: lane_shift 13.2, drift 6.4, seam_drift 4.2, stationary 3.0, emergence 1.05, reversal 0.97. A spot check on 3 seeds showed KDE observation noise is not the driver in drift or lane_shift (it matters in stationary).
Consequence: "geometric structure alone did not improve these placements" goes beyond the evidence. Disclose now. Any change belongs in a new preregistered variant (for example, use only backtests where stacking fits weights). The C3 residual candidate has the same pooled factor at target 5.


P3 (Low). Concentrated-mass test.


Where: tests/test_research_extensions.py:147-154.
Proposal: the test checks only the bound from the independent coupling. Assert agreement with an exact OT solver (I measured 1.3e-10).


P4 (Low). Sampler RNG.


Proposal: add an opt-in RNG for the component sampler seeded per replicate, and keep the current default.


5. The four areas, kept separate


Implementation correctness. Strong for what was asked. The sampling laws, chronological fitting, AIS anchoring, fallback path, TV and W1 starts, LP and dual, gate code and summary chain are all correct, and everything I regenerated was bit-identical. The only defects are D3 (recording) and D4 (latent).
Experimental evidence. C1 is synthetic and supports "better than pooled history, not better than the best simple component". C4/C6 rest on one reused day: 4 overlapping origins, 19 to 54 test pieces each, and a scale at the edge of the grid. C5 is a small grid with 15 candidates, few distinct plans, and radii driven by a pooled fallback. None of this supports any claim about operational or population performance, and the repository mostly says so.
Calibration. None is established. The control sampler and both candidates fail the frozen gate. The diagnostics measure latent expected loss on the planning grid, not realized-count PIT or the full predictive law, and they share common random numbers across seeds.
Novelty. The repository makes no novelty claim, and the evidence does not support one. Most components are established methods. The transport ambiguity needs citations (D13). The specific line-space metric and Poisson intensity stacking for line processes may be new combinations, but I did not run a literature search thorough enough to say so.
